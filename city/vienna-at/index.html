The climate of Vienna in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 26°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -5°.
  • Vienna gets about 725 mm of rain a year, on roughly 134 days; the wettest month is July (90 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are May – September.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in December – March.
