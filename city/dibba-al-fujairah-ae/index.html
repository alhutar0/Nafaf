The climate of Dibba Al-Fujairah in brief

  • The warmest month is June, with an average high of 40°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 17°.
  • Dibba Al-Fujairah gets about 118 mm of rain a year, on roughly 19 days; the wettest month is January (30 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are November – March.
  • Summer heat is extreme: afternoons above 40° are normal in June.
