The climate of Beijing in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 32°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -10°.
  • Beijing gets about 535 mm of rain a year, on roughly 68 days; the wettest month is July (164 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in June – August.
  • The most comfortable months are April and May and August – October.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in November – March.
