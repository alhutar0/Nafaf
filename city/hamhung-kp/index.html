The climate of Hamhung in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 27°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -12°.
  • Hamhung gets about 882 mm of rain a year, on roughly 96 days; the wettest month is July (222 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in June – September.
  • The most comfortable months are May and June and September.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in November – March.
