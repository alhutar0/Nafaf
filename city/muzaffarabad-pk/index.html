The climate of Muzaffarabad in brief

  • The warmest month is June, with an average high of 27°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -5°.
  • Muzaffarabad gets about 797 mm of rain a year, on roughly 105 days; the wettest month is August (122 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are May and June and September and October.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in December – March.
