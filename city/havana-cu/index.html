The climate of Havana in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 33°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 19°.
  • Havana gets about 785 mm of rain a year, on roughly 113 days; the wettest month is September (117 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are October – March.
