The climate of Dammam in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 42°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 13°.
  • Rain is rare in Dammam: about 54 mm a year in total.
  • The most comfortable months are November – March.
  • Summer heat is extreme: afternoons above 40° are normal in June – August.
