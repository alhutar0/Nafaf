The climate of Qurayyat in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 38°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 2°.
  • Rain is rare in Qurayyat: about 55 mm a year in total.
  • The most comfortable months are March and April and October and November.
