The climate of Mecca in brief

  • The warmest month is June, with an average high of 43°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 16°.
  • Rain is rare in Mecca: about 32 mm a year in total.
  • The most comfortable months are December – February.
  • Summer heat is extreme: afternoons above 40° are normal in May – September.
