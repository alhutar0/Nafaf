The climate of Saint John’s in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 29°.
  • The coolest month is February, with an average low of 25°.
  • Saint John’s gets about 1072 mm of rain a year, on roughly 194 days; the wettest month is October (157 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are January – April.
