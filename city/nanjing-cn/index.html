The climate of Nanjing in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 32°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -2°.
  • Nanjing gets about 1142 mm of rain a year, on roughly 114 days; the wettest month is July (214 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are April and May and September and October.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in December – February.
