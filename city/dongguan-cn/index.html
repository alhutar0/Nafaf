The climate of Dongguan in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 31°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 9°.
  • Dongguan gets about 1663 mm of rain a year, on roughly 154 days; the wettest month is June (325 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in May – August.
  • The most comfortable months are October – March.
