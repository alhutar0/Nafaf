The climate of Gent in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 23°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 0°.
  • Gent gets about 779 mm of rain a year, on roughly 155 days; the wettest month is December (80 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are June – September.
