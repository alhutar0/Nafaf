The climate of Kota Bharu in brief

  • The warmest month is May, with an average high of 31°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 24°.
  • Kota Bharu gets about 2531 mm of rain a year, on roughly 272 days; the wettest month is November (520 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are February and March.
