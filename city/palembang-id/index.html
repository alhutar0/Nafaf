The climate of Palembang in brief

  • The warmest month is October, with an average high of 31°.
  • The coolest month is August, with an average low of 22°.
  • Palembang gets about 2368 mm of rain a year, on roughly 232 days; the wettest month is March (306 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are June – September.
