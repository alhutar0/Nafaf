The climate of Charlotte Amalie in brief

  • The warmest month is September, with an average high of 29°.
  • The coolest month is February, with an average low of 25°.
  • Charlotte Amalie gets about 1103 mm of rain a year, on roughly 231 days; the wettest month is September (160 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are January – April.
