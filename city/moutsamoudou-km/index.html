The climate of Moutsamoudou in brief

  • The warmest month is March, with an average high of 28°.
  • The coolest month is August, with an average low of 24°.
  • Moutsamoudou gets about 1008 mm of rain a year, on roughly 170 days; the wettest month is January (212 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in January – April.
  • The most comfortable months are May – November.
