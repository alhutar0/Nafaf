The climate of Mashhad in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 31°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -5°.
  • Mashhad gets about 177 mm of rain a year, on roughly 40 days; the wettest month is March (38 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are April – June and August – October.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in December – March.
