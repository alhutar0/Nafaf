The climate of Nantes in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 25°.
  • The coolest month is February, with an average low of 2°.
  • Nantes gets about 794 mm of rain a year, on roughly 136 days; the wettest month is December (92 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are May – September.
