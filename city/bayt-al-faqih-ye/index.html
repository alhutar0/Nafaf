The climate of Bayt al Faqīh in brief

  • The warmest month is June, with an average high of 37°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 23°.
  • Bayt al Faqīh gets about 281 mm of rain a year, on roughly 49 days; the wettest month is August (61 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are December – February.
