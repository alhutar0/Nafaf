The climate of Adelaide in brief

  • The warmest month is January, with an average high of 29°.
  • The coolest month is July, with an average low of 6°.
  • Adelaide gets about 456 mm of rain a year, on roughly 87 days; the wettest month is June (64 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are October – May.
