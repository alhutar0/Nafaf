The climate of Dallas in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 97°F.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 34°F.
  • Dallas gets about 956 mm of rain a year, on roughly 107 days; the wettest month is May (116 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are March – May and October and November.
