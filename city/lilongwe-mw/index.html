The climate of Lilongwe in brief

  • The warmest month is October, with an average high of 31°.
  • The coolest month is July, with an average low of 10°.
  • Lilongwe gets about 1046 mm of rain a year, on roughly 127 days; the wettest month is January (299 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in December – February.
  • The most comfortable months are April – September and November.
