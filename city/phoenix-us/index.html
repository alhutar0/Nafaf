The climate of Phoenix in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 105°F.
  • The coolest month is December, with an average low of 41°F.
  • Phoenix gets about 280 mm of rain a year, on roughly 49 days; the wettest month is January (37 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are November – April.
  • Summer heat is extreme: afternoons above 40° are normal in June and July.
