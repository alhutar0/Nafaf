The climate of Houston in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 95°F.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 41°F.
  • Houston gets about 1283 mm of rain a year, on roughly 138 days; the wettest month is October (130 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are February – May and October and November.
