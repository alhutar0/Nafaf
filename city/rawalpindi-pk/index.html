The climate of Rawalpindi in brief

  • The warmest month is June, with an average high of 41°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 6°.
  • Rawalpindi gets about 895 mm of rain a year, on roughly 90 days; the wettest month is July (202 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in March and June – August.
  • The most comfortable months are October – March.
  • Summer heat is extreme: afternoons above 40° are normal in June.
