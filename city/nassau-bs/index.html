The climate of Nassau in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 85°F.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 72°F.
  • Nassau gets about 1044 mm of rain a year, on roughly 158 days; the wettest month is June (156 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are November – May.
