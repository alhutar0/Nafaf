The climate of The Bronx in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 84°F.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 21°F.
  • The Bronx gets about 1208 mm of rain a year, on roughly 131 days; the wettest month is October (110 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are May – September.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in December – March.
