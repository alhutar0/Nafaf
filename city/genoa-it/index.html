The climate of Genoa in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 28°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 1°.
  • Genoa gets about 876 mm of rain a year, on roughly 107 days; the wettest month is November (138 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are May – October.
