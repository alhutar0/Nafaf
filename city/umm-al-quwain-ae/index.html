The climate of Umm al-Quwain in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 40°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 18°.
  • Umm al-Quwain gets about 102 mm of rain a year, on roughly 17 days; the wettest month is January (26 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are November – March.
  • Summer heat is extreme: afternoons above 40° are normal in July and August.
