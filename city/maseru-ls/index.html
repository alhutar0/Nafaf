The climate of Maseru in brief

  • The warmest month is January, with an average high of 28°.
  • The coolest month is July, with an average low of 1°.
  • Maseru gets about 684 mm of rain a year, on roughly 110 days; the wettest month is January (106 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are March – May and September – November.
