The climate of Khartoum in brief

  • The warmest month is May, with an average high of 42°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 14°.
  • Khartoum gets about 140 mm of rain a year, on roughly 32 days; the wettest month is August (58 mm).
  • No month is fully mild here; the least extreme are December – February.
  • Summer heat is extreme: afternoons above 40° are normal in April – June.
