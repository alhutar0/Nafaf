The climate of Battagram in brief

  • The warmest month is June, with an average high of 33°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 0°.
  • Battagram gets about 791 mm of rain a year, on roughly 99 days; the wettest month is July (137 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are April and May and September – November.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in January.
