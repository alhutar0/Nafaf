The climate of Brno in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 27°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -6°.
  • Brno gets about 588 mm of rain a year, on roughly 114 days; the wettest month is July (73 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are May – September.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in December – March.
