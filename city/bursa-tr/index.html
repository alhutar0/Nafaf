The climate of Bursa in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 31°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -1°.
  • Bursa gets about 630 mm of rain a year, on roughly 99 days; the wettest month is December (86 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are May – October.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in January.
