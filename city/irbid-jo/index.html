The climate of Irbid in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 39°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 5°.
  • Irbid gets about 372 mm of rain a year, on roughly 52 days; the wettest month is January (91 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are February – April and November and December.
