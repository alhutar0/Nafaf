The climate of Al Muharraq in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 35°.
  • The coolest month is February, with an average low of 18°.
  • Al Muharraq gets about 80 mm of rain a year, on roughly 14 days; the wettest month is November (17 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are October – May.
