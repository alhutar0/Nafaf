The climate of Los Angeles in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 87°F.
  • The coolest month is December, with an average low of 46°F.
  • Los Angeles gets about 372 mm of rain a year, on roughly 36 days; the wettest month is February (93 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are March – November.
