The climate of Fortaleza in brief

  • The warmest month is November, with an average high of 30°.
  • The coolest month is July, with an average low of 24°.
  • Fortaleza gets about 1091 mm of rain a year, on roughly 149 days; the wettest month is April (258 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in February – May.
  • The most comfortable months are July – December.
