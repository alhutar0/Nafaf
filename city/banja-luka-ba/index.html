The climate of Banja Luka in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 30°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -3°.
  • Banja Luka gets about 860 mm of rain a year, on roughly 129 days; the wettest month is September (88 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are May – September.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in December – February.
