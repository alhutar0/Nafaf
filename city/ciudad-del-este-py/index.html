The climate of Ciudad del Este in brief

  • The warmest month is January, with an average high of 32°.
  • The coolest month is July, with an average low of 12°.
  • Ciudad del Este gets about 1701 mm of rain a year, on roughly 151 days; the wettest month is October (216 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are March and April and June – September.
