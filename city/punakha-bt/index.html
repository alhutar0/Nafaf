The climate of Punakha in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 19°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -4°.
  • Punakha gets about 969 mm of rain a year, on roughly 157 days; the wettest month is July (230 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in June – August.
  • No month is fully mild here; the least extreme are June – August.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in December – March.
