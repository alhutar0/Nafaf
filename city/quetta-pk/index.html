The climate of Quetta in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 34°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -1°.
  • Quetta gets about 170 mm of rain a year, on roughly 27 days; the wettest month is March (30 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are March – May and September – November.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in January.
