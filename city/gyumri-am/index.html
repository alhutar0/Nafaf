The climate of Gyumri in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 24°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -14°.
  • Gyumri gets about 466 mm of rain a year, on roughly 111 days; the wettest month is May (66 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are June – September.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in November – April.
