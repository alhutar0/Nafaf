The climate of Bandar Seri Begawan in brief

  • The warmest month is May, with an average high of 29°.
  • The coolest month is February, with an average low of 25°.
  • Bandar Seri Begawan gets about 2837 mm of rain a year, on roughly 302 days; the wettest month is December (318 mm).
  • No month is fully mild here; the least extreme are January and February and October.
