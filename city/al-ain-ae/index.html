The climate of Al Ain in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 45°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 13°.
  • Rain is rare in Al Ain: about 66 mm a year in total.
  • The most comfortable months are December – February.
  • Summer heat is extreme: afternoons above 40° are normal in May – September.
