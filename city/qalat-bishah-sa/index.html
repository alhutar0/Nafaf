The climate of Qal‘at Bīshah in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 39°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 11°.
  • Rain is rare in Qal‘at Bīshah: about 38 mm a year in total.
  • The most comfortable months are November – February.
