The climate of Jeddah in brief

  • The warmest month is June, with an average high of 41°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 19°.
  • Rain is rare in Jeddah: about 34 mm a year in total.
  • The most comfortable months are December – February.
  • Summer heat is extreme: afternoons above 40° are normal in June – September.
