The climate of Riga in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 21°.
  • The coolest month is February, with an average low of -8°.
  • Riga gets about 771 mm of rain a year, on roughly 159 days; the wettest month is October (79 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are June and August.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in November – March.
