The climate of San Jose in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 87°F.
  • The coolest month is December, with an average low of 39°F.
  • San Jose gets about 548 mm of rain a year, on roughly 62 days; the wettest month is January (109 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in December – March.
  • The most comfortable months are April – November.
