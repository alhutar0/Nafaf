The climate of Port-au-Prince in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 33°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 22°.
  • Port-au-Prince gets about 438 mm of rain a year, on roughly 75 days; the wettest month is May (62 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are November – March.
