The climate of Nagoya in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 30°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 1°.
  • Nagoya gets about 1706 mm of rain a year, on roughly 147 days; the wettest month is September (223 mm).
  • The most comfortable month is April.
