The climate of Cape Town in brief

  • The warmest month is February, with an average high of 22°.
  • The coolest month is August, with an average low of 13°.
  • Cape Town gets about 458 mm of rain a year, on roughly 80 days; the wettest month is June (83 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are November – May.
