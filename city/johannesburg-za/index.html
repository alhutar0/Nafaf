The climate of Johannesburg in brief

  • The warmest month is January, with an average high of 28°.
  • The coolest month is July, with an average low of 3°.
  • Johannesburg gets about 673 mm of rain a year, on roughly 115 days; the wettest month is December (126 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in November – February.
  • The most comfortable months are March – October.
