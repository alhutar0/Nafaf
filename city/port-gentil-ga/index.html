The climate of Port-Gentil in brief

  • The warmest month is March, with an average high of 28°.
  • The coolest month is July, with an average low of 23°.
  • Port-Gentil gets about 1851 mm of rain a year, on roughly 226 days; the wettest month is November (309 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are June – September.
