The climate of Mosul in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 43°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 2°.
  • Mosul gets about 302 mm of rain a year, on roughly 58 days; the wettest month is March (51 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are March and April and October and November.
  • Summer heat is extreme: afternoons above 40° are normal in July and August.
