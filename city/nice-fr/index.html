The climate of Nice in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 25°.
  • The coolest month is February, with an average low of 9°.
  • Nice gets about 695 mm of rain a year, on roughly 85 days; the wettest month is November (115 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are May – October.
