The climate of Edmonton in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 23°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -16°.
  • Edmonton gets about 515 mm of rain a year, on roughly 106 days; the wettest month is June (88 mm).
  • The most comfortable month is August.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in October – April.
