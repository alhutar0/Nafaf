The climate of Lagos in brief

  • The warmest month is February, with an average high of 30°.
  • The coolest month is August, with an average low of 23°.
  • Lagos gets about 1477 mm of rain a year, on roughly 223 days; the wettest month is June (242 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are November – February.
