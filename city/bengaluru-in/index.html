The climate of Bengaluru in brief

  • The warmest month is April, with an average high of 36°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 14°.
  • Bengaluru gets about 859 mm of rain a year, on roughly 115 days; the wettest month is September (161 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in May and August – October.
  • The most comfortable months are November – January and June and July.
