The climate of Minsk in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 22°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -10°.
  • Minsk gets about 748 mm of rain a year, on roughly 149 days; the wettest month is July (100 mm).
  • The most comfortable month is August.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in November – March.
