The climate of Az Zulfī in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 43°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 6°.
  • Rain is rare in Az Zulfī: about 37 mm a year in total.
  • The most comfortable months are November – March.
  • Summer heat is extreme: afternoons above 40° are normal in June – September.
