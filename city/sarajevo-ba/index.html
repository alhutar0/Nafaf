The climate of Sarajevo in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 26°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -5°.
  • Sarajevo gets about 870 mm of rain a year, on roughly 143 days; the wettest month is November (95 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are June – September.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in December – March.
