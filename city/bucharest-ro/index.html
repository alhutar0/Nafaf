The climate of Bucharest in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 32°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -5°.
  • Bucharest gets about 563 mm of rain a year, on roughly 100 days; the wettest month is June (71 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are April – June and September and October.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in December – February.
