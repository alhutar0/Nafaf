The climate of Luganville in brief

  • The warmest month is January, with an average high of 29°.
  • The coolest month is August, with an average low of 23°.
  • Luganville gets about 2210 mm of rain a year, on roughly 220 days; the wettest month is March (292 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are July – September.
