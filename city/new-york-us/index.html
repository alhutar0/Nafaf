The climate of New York in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 79°F.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 29°F.
  • New York gets about 1256 mm of rain a year, on roughly 131 days; the wettest month is December (121 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are June – September.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in January and February.
