The climate of Santo Domingo in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 32°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 21°.
  • Santo Domingo gets about 526 mm of rain a year, on roughly 101 days; the wettest month is August (61 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are October – March and May.
