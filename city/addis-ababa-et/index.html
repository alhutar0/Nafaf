The climate of Addis Ababa in brief

  • The warmest month is March, with an average high of 26°.
  • The coolest month is December, with an average low of 7°.
  • Addis Ababa gets about 1120 mm of rain a year, on roughly 182 days; the wettest month is August (256 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in June – September.
  • The most comfortable months are October – March.
