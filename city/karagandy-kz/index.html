The climate of Karagandy in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 26°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -20°.
  • Karagandy gets about 350 mm of rain a year, on roughly 88 days; the wettest month is July (46 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are May – September.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in October – April.
