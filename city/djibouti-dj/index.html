The climate of Djibouti in brief

  • The warmest month is June, with an average high of 39°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 22°.
  • Djibouti gets about 267 mm of rain a year, on roughly 55 days; the wettest month is April (48 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are November – March.
