The climate of Douala in brief

  • The warmest month is February, with an average high of 32°.
  • The coolest month is August, with an average low of 22°.
  • Douala gets about 2394 mm of rain a year, on roughly 273 days; the wettest month is August (369 mm).
  • The most comfortable month is December.
