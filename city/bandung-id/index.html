The climate of Bandung in brief

  • The warmest month is October, with an average high of 27°.
  • The coolest month is August, with an average low of 16°.
  • Bandung gets about 2450 mm of rain a year, on roughly 248 days; the wettest month is February (327 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are June – September.
