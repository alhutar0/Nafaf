The climate of Bājil in brief

  • The warmest month is June, with an average high of 38°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 22°.
  • Bājil gets about 148 mm of rain a year, on roughly 30 days; the wettest month is August (32 mm).
  • The most comfortable month is January.
