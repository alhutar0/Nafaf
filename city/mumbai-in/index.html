The climate of Mumbai in brief

  • The warmest month is April, with an average high of 39°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 16°.
  • Mumbai gets about 2342 mm of rain a year, on roughly 121 days; the wettest month is July (793 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in June – August.
  • No month is fully mild here; the least extreme are July – September.
