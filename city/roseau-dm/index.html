The climate of Roseau in brief

  • The warmest month is September, with an average high of 28°.
  • The coolest month is February, with an average low of 24°.
  • Roseau gets about 1412 mm of rain a year, on roughly 241 days; the wettest month is October (183 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are February and March.
