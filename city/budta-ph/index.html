The climate of Budta in brief

  • The warmest month is April, with an average high of 33°.
  • The coolest month is February, with an average low of 21°.
  • Budta gets about 2073 mm of rain a year, on roughly 244 days; the wettest month is June (284 mm).
  • No month is fully mild here; the least extreme are July and September and October.
