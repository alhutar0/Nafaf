The climate of Puebla City in brief

  • The warmest month is April, with an average high of 28°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 7°.
  • Puebla City gets about 909 mm of rain a year, on roughly 162 days; the wettest month is September (178 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in June – September.
  • The most comfortable months are November – April.
