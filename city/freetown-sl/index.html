The climate of Freetown in brief

  • The warmest month is March, with an average high of 32°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 22°.
  • Freetown gets about 2560 mm of rain a year, on roughly 165 days; the wettest month is August (665 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in July – September.
  • The most comfortable months are November – February.
