The climate of San Antonio in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 97°F.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 40°F.
  • San Antonio gets about 822 mm of rain a year, on roughly 98 days; the wettest month is May (105 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are February – April and October and November.
