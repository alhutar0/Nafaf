The climate of Córdoba in brief

  • The warmest month is January, with an average high of 31°.
  • The coolest month is July, with an average low of 4°.
  • Córdoba gets about 823 mm of rain a year, on roughly 88 days; the wettest month is December (132 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in November – February.
