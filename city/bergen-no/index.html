The climate of Bergen in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 17°.
  • The coolest month is February, with an average low of -4°.
  • Bergen gets about 2714 mm of rain a year, on roughly 242 days; the wettest month is December (313 mm).
  • No month is fully mild here; the least extreme are June – August.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in November – March.
