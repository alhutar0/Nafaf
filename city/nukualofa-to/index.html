The climate of Nuku‘alofa in brief

  • The warmest month is February, with an average high of 27°.
  • The coolest month is August, with an average low of 22°.
  • Nuku‘alofa gets about 1486 mm of rain a year, on roughly 182 days; the wettest month is March (193 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are May – November.
