The climate of Kandahar in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 41°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 1°.
  • Kandahar gets about 158 mm of rain a year, on roughly 23 days; the wettest month is March (27 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are March and April and October and November.
  • Summer heat is extreme: afternoons above 40° are normal in July.
