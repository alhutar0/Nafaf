The climate of Gold Coast in brief

  • The warmest month is January, with an average high of 30°.
  • The coolest month is July, with an average low of 10°.
  • Gold Coast gets about 1163 mm of rain a year, on roughly 139 days; the wettest month is February (164 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are April – January.
