The climate of Ho Chi Minh City in brief

  • The warmest month is March, with an average high of 38°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 21°.
  • Ho Chi Minh City gets about 1468 mm of rain a year, on roughly 165 days; the wettest month is July (226 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in June – August and October.
  • The most comfortable months are November and December.
