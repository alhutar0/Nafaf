The climate of Colombo in brief

  • The warmest month is March, with an average high of 32°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 22°.
  • Colombo gets about 1775 mm of rain a year, on roughly 240 days; the wettest month is November (266 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are January and February.
