The climate of Athens in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 32°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 7°.
  • Athens gets about 477 mm of rain a year, on roughly 74 days; the wettest month is December (81 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are April – June and September – November.
