The climate of Limassol in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 30°.
  • The coolest month is February, with an average low of 12°.
  • Limassol gets about 383 mm of rain a year, on roughly 66 days; the wettest month is December (90 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are April – November.
