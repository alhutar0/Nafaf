The climate of Yokohama in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 30°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -2°.
  • Yokohama gets about 1584 mm of rain a year, on roughly 136 days; the wettest month is September (215 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are May and August.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in January and February.
