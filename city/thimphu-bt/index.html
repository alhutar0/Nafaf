The climate of Thimphu in brief

  • The warmest month is June, with an average high of 17°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -7°.
  • Thimphu gets about 928 mm of rain a year, on roughly 150 days; the wettest month is July (232 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in June – August.
  • No month is fully mild here; the least extreme are June – August.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in November – April.
