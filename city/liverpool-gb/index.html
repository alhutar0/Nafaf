The climate of Liverpool in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 18°.
  • The coolest month is February, with an average low of 3°.
  • Liverpool gets about 965 mm of rain a year, on roughly 192 days; the wettest month is December (101 mm).
  • No month is fully mild here; the least extreme are June – August.
