The climate of Misratah in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 30°.
  • The coolest month is February, with an average low of 12°.
  • Misratah gets about 259 mm of rain a year, on roughly 46 days; the wettest month is December (48 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are March – December.
