The climate of Al Farwaniyah in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 42°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 10°.
  • Rain is rare in Al Farwaniyah: about 66 mm a year in total.
  • The most comfortable months are February – April and November and December.
  • Summer heat is extreme: afternoons above 40° are normal in June – August.
