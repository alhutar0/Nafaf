The climate of Bulawayo in brief

  • The warmest month is October, with an average high of 32°.
  • The coolest month is July, with an average low of 7°.
  • Bulawayo gets about 625 mm of rain a year, on roughly 92 days; the wettest month is December (136 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in December – February.
  • The most comfortable months are February – September and November.
