The climate of Almaty in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 34°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -5°.
  • Almaty gets about 450 mm of rain a year, on roughly 83 days; the wettest month is April (70 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are April – June and September and October.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in December – February.
