The climate of Dubai in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 40°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 18°.
  • Dubai gets about 91 mm of rain a year, on roughly 15 days; the wettest month is January (24 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are November – April.
