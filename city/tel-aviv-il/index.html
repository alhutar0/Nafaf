The climate of Tel Aviv in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 34°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 8°.
  • Tel Aviv gets about 332 mm of rain a year, on roughly 48 days; the wettest month is January (82 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are March – May and October – December.
