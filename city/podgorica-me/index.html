The climate of Podgorica in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 28°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -4°.
  • Podgorica gets about 1321 mm of rain a year, on roughly 128 days; the wettest month is November (186 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are June – September.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in December – March.
