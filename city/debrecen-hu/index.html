The climate of Debrecen in brief

  • The warmest month is August, with an average high of 30°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -5°.
  • Debrecen gets about 589 mm of rain a year, on roughly 116 days; the wettest month is July (72 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are May – September.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in December – February.
