The climate of Bisee in brief

  • The warmest month is September, with an average high of 29°.
  • The coolest month is February, with an average low of 25°.
  • Bisee gets about 1657 mm of rain a year, on roughly 211 days; the wettest month is October (210 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are February – April.
