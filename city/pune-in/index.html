The climate of Pune in brief

  • The warmest month is April, with an average high of 38°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of 12°.
  • Pune gets about 1617 mm of rain a year, on roughly 120 days; the wettest month is July (489 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in June – August.
  • The most comfortable months are October – January.
