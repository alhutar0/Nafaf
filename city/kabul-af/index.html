The climate of Kabul in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 32°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -5°.
  • Kabul gets about 424 mm of rain a year, on roughly 64 days; the wettest month is March (63 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are April – June and August – October.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in December – February.
