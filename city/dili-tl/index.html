The climate of Dili in brief

  • The warmest month is October, with an average high of 33°.
  • The coolest month is August, with an average low of 22°.
  • Dili gets about 941 mm of rain a year, on roughly 102 days; the wettest month is January (229 mm).
  • Most of the year's rain falls in December – February.
  • The most comfortable months are March – August.
