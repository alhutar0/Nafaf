The climate of Graz in brief

  • The warmest month is July, with an average high of 27°.
  • The coolest month is January, with an average low of -4°.
  • Graz gets about 820 mm of rain a year, on roughly 128 days; the wettest month is July (107 mm).
  • The most comfortable months are May and August and September.
  • Nights fall below freezing on average in December – March.
